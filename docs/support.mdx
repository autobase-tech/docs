# Support

We provide personalized support and expert assistance, so you can focus on building your project with confidence, knowing you have a reliable partner to help with your PostgreSQL operations.

Paid plans include monthly DBA consulting hours and a private Slack channel. Included hours and support response times depend on your plan.

:::tip Support inquiries
For support inquiries, please contact us at <a href="mailto:support@autobase.tech">support@autobase.tech</a>
:::
